# The Obsidian Room — Luxury Grooming Lounge & Bespoke Salon

An ultra-premium, cinematic web experience crafted for **The Obsidian Room**, situated on Beach Road in Visakhapatnam.

---

## ✦ Aesthetics & Design Philosophy

- **Vibe:** Exclusive, moody, editorial, bespoke — inspired by haute horlogerie, luxury fashion houses, and discreet five-star hospitality.
- **Palette:**
  - **Matte Black (`#0A0A0A`)** & **Deep Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`)**
  - **Muted Gold (`#C5A059`)** & **Brushed Copper (`#D49B5B`)**
  - **Crisp Off-White (`#F3F4F6`)** & **Slate Gray (`#9CA3AF`)**
- **Typography:** *Playfair Display* & *Cormorant Garamond* serifs with *Plus Jakarta Sans* geometric sans-serif.
- **Atmospheric Sound Synthesizer:** Built-in Web Audio API generator delivering warm analog lounge room acoustic tone and tactile golden feedback clicks.
